About SmartKit

Welcome to SmartKit, your online destination for simple and useful digital tools.

SmartKit brings different everyday tools together in one convenient place. Our goal is to make common digital tasks easier, faster and more accessible.

What We Offer

  • PDF tools for merging, splitting and compressing files.
  • Image tools for compressing, resizing and converting images.
  • QR Code Generator.
  • Text tools for counting, converting and repeating text.
  • Calculators for percentages and age.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide easy-to-use online tools that help people complete everyday digital tasks without unnecessary complications.

Free and Easy to Use

We aim to keep SmartKit simple, accessible and useful for everyone.
